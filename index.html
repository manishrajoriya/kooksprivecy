02

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate, improve, and personalize Hii Looks. Specifically:

  • To process your selfies and generate AI-powered attractiveness ratings and personalized advice
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services and features
  • To manage your account, subscription, and billing via Google Play
  • To send you service-related notifications (results ready, subscription renewals)
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve AI model accuracy
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your photos, face data, or personal information with third-party advertisers for commercial targeting.
03

Camera & Face Data

Hii Looksrequires access to your device's camera or photo library to perform facial analysis. Here's exactly how we handle this sensitive data:

⚡ Important: Your photos are sent to our secure AI processing servers solely to generate your attractiveness rating and trait scores. We do not build facial recognition databases, biometric profiles, or sell face data to any third party.
  • Processing only: Photos are analyzed in real-time and results are returned. The raw image is deleted from our servers within 24 hours of processing.
  • No facial recognition: We do not use your images for identity verification or match them against any database of individuals.
  • No training without consent: We will not use your photos to train AI models without your explicit opt-in consent.
  • Local storage: Your scan results (scores and advice text) may be stored locally on your device and/or in your account for history purposes.
  • Camera permission: The app requests camera access only when you initiate a scan. You may deny this permission and use photo library upload instead.

Third-Party Services

Hii Looksintegrates with trusted third-party services to deliver our features. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Billing — Handles subscription payments. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We ensure all third-party partners are contractually obligated to protect your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

05

Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement industry-standard measures:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Our servers are hosted on Google Cloud Platform with enterprise-grade security
  • We conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access to user data is restricted to essential personnel only, under strict access controls
While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but commit to notifying you promptly in the event of a data breach affecting your information.

Data Retention

  • Uploaded photos: Deleted from servers within 24 hours of AI processing completion

You may delete your account at any time via Settings → Account → Delete Account within the app, or by contacting us directly.
