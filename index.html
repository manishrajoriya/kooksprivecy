Privacy Policy for Step Lock

Effective Date: July 15, 2026

Step Lock respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application uses permissions and handles information.

Information We Access

  • Physical Activity / Step Count: Used to count your steps and calculate app unlock time.
  • Usage Access Permission: Used to detect app usage time and manage app locking and unlocking.
  • Display Over Other Apps Permission: Used to show the lock screen when an app is restricted.
  • Installed Apps Information: Used only to display the list of apps on your device so you can select which apps you want to lock.

How We Use This Information

  • Track steps completed by the user.
  • Convert steps into unlock time for selected applications.
  • Show a lock screen when access time has expired.
  • Allow users to choose which apps should be controlled by Step Lock.

Data Storage

Step Lock does not collect, store, upload, sell, or share personal information with us or any third parties.

All app settings, step progress, and selected applications remain on your device only.

Third-Party Services

Step Lock does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Step Lock does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

Security

Since your data stays on your device, you maintain control over your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
